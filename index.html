Emre Kuru

PhD Student in Computer Science · Institut Polytechnique de Paris

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at Institut Polytechnique de Paris, working on self-evolving AI agents and agent memory. My research asks how long-horizon agents can acquire, organize, retrieve, and improve procedural knowledge from experience.

Research interests

  • Self-evolving agents and procedural memory
  • Long-horizon LLM agents
  • Information retrieval and multimodal RAG
